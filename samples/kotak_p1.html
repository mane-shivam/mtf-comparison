Margin Trading Stock List on Kotak Neo

With the Kotak Neo MTF stock list, you can explore over 1500+ eligible stocks for the Margin Trading Facility (MTF), giving you up to 4x leverage to amplify your trading potential. Updated daily, this curated list ensures you never miss opportunities in high-quality, liquid stocks.

By providing a ready reference of approved stocks, the MTF stock list enables faster, more informed decision-making while keeping you in control of your capital and risk management.

What is the Margin Trading Stock List?

The MTF stock list consists of a select group of stocks that have been allowed to be traded on margin by the broker or exchange. These stocks have undergone vetting for liquidity, volatility, and market capitalisation, among other metrics, to ensure their appropriateness for margin trading. Since the list updates daily, it reflects the most current opportunities and helps you act quickly in dynamic market conditions.

The MTF stock list acts like a reference for approved stocks. By knowing which stocks qualify for margin trading upfront, you can focus on timing, strategy, and execution while staying in control of your capital and risk exposure.

How to Use the MTF Stock List?

You can access the MTF stock list directly on the Kotak Neo website and easily search a stock and check if it is eligible for MTF. Here’s how you can make the most of the MTF stock list:

  • Search quickly: Type in the stock name or NSE symbol to confirm MTF eligibility.
  • Scan key details: View the scrip name, NSE symbol, margin multiple, and maximum allowed exposure.
  • Check daily: Since the list is updated every trading day, reviewing it regularly ensures you’re acting on the latest information.
  • Manage risk: Use disciplined stop-loss and exit strategies when trading with leverage.

Align the MTF stock list with your own trading strategy, considering factors such as price range, sector, and preferred leverage levels. This way, it becomes not just a list but a practical starting point for building well-timed, well-informed trades.

*Max allowed exposure reflects the combined value of stocks pledged and those purchased through Pay Later (MTF).
